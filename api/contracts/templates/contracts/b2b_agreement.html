1. Preamble

The First Party operates MeritLense, designed to provide secure, neutral, and scalable tools for evaluating non-academic workforce profiles prior to employment. The Platform specializes in operational, technical, and non-academic competency assessment — and does not provide recruitment, brokerage, or legal representation services.

The Second Party wishes to access these services to support its business activities under the terms set out in this Agreement. This preamble shall be considered an integral part of this Agreement.

2. Scope of Services

The Platform provides the Second Party with two evaluation tiers, based on the selected package:

Screening Evaluation

  • Quick assessment to support proceed / no-proceed decisions
  • Identity verification and foundational assessment across the approved dimensions
  • Preliminary Readiness Indicator using the same official labels (Ready / Partially Ready / Readiness Gaps Identified) as a preliminary signal only — not equivalent to the Full Evaluation's official Workforce Readiness Level, and not a certificate-eligible output

Full MeritLense Evaluation

  • Comprehensive assessment across the five approved Workforce Readiness dimensions: Safety Awareness, Hygiene & Standards, Communication Ability, Practical Task Execution, and Behavioral Indicators
  • Detailed report + official Workforce Readiness Level + candidate comparison
  • AI-Led or Employer-Led assessment path, as applicable to the selected package

Included platform services

  • Multilingual dashboards for candidate comparison
  • Technical support, account configuration, and onboarding
  • Optional integration with internal systems (subject to a separate agreement)

The Platform does not participate in recruitment, candidate selection, or legal representation. Its role is strictly limited to providing technical assessment tools. The Platform may not be used to evaluate academic or specialized professional roles (such as doctors, engineers, or lawyers). Use is restricted to operational, technical, and non-academic workforce sectors.

3. Responsibilities of the Parties

First Party — MeritLense

  • Ensure secure and reliable platform operation
  • Provide technical support and onboarding assistance
  • Protect user data in accordance with the GDPR and Estonian data protection laws
  • Maintain platform neutrality and full legal compliance
  • Provide objective evaluation reports based on neutral data and algorithms

Second Party — Client

  • Provide accurate, up-to-date job profiles and clear evaluation requests
  • Comply with applicable labor, immigration, and professional ethics standards in its jurisdiction
  • Keep account information current and secure
  • Ensure fees collected from third parties align with local regulatory guidelines
  • Use the Platform solely for lawful and professional purposes within the targeted operational sectors
  • Not resell evaluation reports or results to third parties, or use them outside the agreed employment scope

5. Refund Policy (B2B)

The current paid billing period is not refundable once payment is confirmed, regardless of usage. A Second Party wishing to stop future billing must cancel the subscription; cancellation does not refund the current period, and the account may continue using its existing allocation until the end of that period.

Exceptionally, a refund or billing correction may be considered only if:

  • A confirmed billing, payment, or platform error by MeritLense is identified (treated as a billing correction, not a standard refund)
  • A material breach by the First Party of its contractual obligations is proven
  • For Custom Enterprise accounts, the signed Pricing Addendum contains an explicit clause permitting refunds

Refund requests must be submitted in writing to info@meritlense.com. If approved, refunds will be processed within 30 business days, subject to administrative deductions.

7. Intellectual Property & Confidentiality

  • The Platform retains all intellectual property rights unless explicitly transferred in writing

8. Acceptable Use Policy

Permitted use is limited to:

  • Evaluating candidates for operational, technical, and non-academic roles exclusively
  • Using reports internally to support direct hiring decisions
  • Sharing evaluation results with the candidate themselves or the direct employer only

Prohibited uses include, but are not limited to:

  • Evaluating academic or specialized professional roles (doctors, engineers, lawyers, etc.)
  • Reselling reports or evaluation results to any third party
  • Using data for discriminatory or unethical targeting purposes
  • Any use that conflicts with applicable labor and privacy laws

9. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be held liable for delays or failures in performance resulting from events beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to: natural disasters, pandemics, wars, governmental decisions, or major electronic infrastructure failures. The affected party shall notify the other party in writing as soon as reasonably possible, and both parties shall endeavor to resume their obligations as soon as the cause is removed.

10. Term and Termination

  • This Agreement commences on the date of signature by both parties and remains valid until terminated under the following conditions
  • Either party may terminate the Agreement with 90 days' prior written notice
  • Upon termination: personally identifying data is deleted or anonymized per the Privacy Policy and the DPA, the underlying assessment records are retained in de-identified form for audit purposes, and outstanding payments are settled amicably

11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of Estonia. Any disputes shall be resolved by the competent courts in Estonia, unless both parties agree in writing on an alternative dispute resolution mechanism (such as international arbitration).

  • All figures and statistics in marketing materials are based on market assumptions and operational estimates
  • The Platform assumes no liability for hiring decisions made by the Second Party based on evaluation results
