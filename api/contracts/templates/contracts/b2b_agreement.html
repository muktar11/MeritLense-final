1. Preamble

MeritLense operates a digital workforce-readiness assessment platform designed to provide structured, auditable and scalable tools for evaluating operational, technical and non-academic workforce competencies before employment. MeritLense provides assessment and decision-support tools only and does not act as a recruitment agency, manpower supplier, employment broker, employer, immigration adviser, or legal representative.

The Second Party wishes to access the Platform to support its lawful workforce assessment and hiring processes. Final employment, hiring, immigration, licensing and deployment decisions remain with the responsible employer and/or appropriately licensed parties.

This Preamble forms an integral part of this Agreement.

2. Scope of Services

The Platform may provide Screening Evaluation and Full MeritLense Evaluation services according to the selected package and role configuration.

Screening Evaluation may include a rapid preliminary assessment, identity-related controls where enabled, and a preliminary readiness signal. Any preliminary readiness signal is for screening support only, is not equivalent to the official Workforce Readiness Level produced by a Full Evaluation, and is not certificate-eligible unless expressly stated by the Platform.

Full MeritLense Evaluation may assess the canonical Workforce Readiness competency framework: Safety Awareness; Hygiene & Standards; Communication Ability; Practical Task Execution; and Behavioral Indicators. Role-specific configuration determines which competencies are Required, Optional, or Not Applicable, together with applicable thresholds, evidence requirements and readiness rules.

Full Evaluation outputs may include a detailed assessment report, an official Workforce Readiness Level, candidate comparison functionality, and certificate eligibility where the applicable assessment and certificate rules are satisfied.

Assessment paths may be AI-assisted and/or employer-led according to the selected package and configuration. MeritLense uses AI-assisted evidence processing where applicable; deterministic platform rules and approved role configuration govern scoring and readiness outputs, while the employer remains the final decision maker.

Included services may also include multilingual dashboards, technical support, account configuration, onboarding and optional integrations subject to separate written agreement.

The Platform does not itself recruit, select, employ, sponsor, deploy or legally represent candidates.
