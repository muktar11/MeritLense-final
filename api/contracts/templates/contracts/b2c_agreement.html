1. Preamble

The First Party operates MeritLense, designed to provide secure, neutral, and scalable tools for evaluating operational, technical, and non-academic competencies prior to employment. The Platform specializes in serving individuals and household employers seeking an objective and structured candidate assessment.

The Second Party (individual / household employer) wishes to access these services in a personal capacity for the purpose of evaluating a candidate to work in or for their household. This Agreement does not create any employment, brokerage, or legal representation relationship on the part of the Platform. This preamble shall be considered an integral part of this Agreement.

2. Scope of Services

The Platform provides the Second Party with two evaluation tiers, based on the selected package:

Screening Evaluation

  • Quick assessment to support proceed / no-proceed decisions
  • Identity verification and foundational assessment across the approved dimensions
  • Preliminary Readiness Indicator using the same official labels (Ready / Partially Ready / Readiness Gaps Identified) as a preliminary signal only — not equivalent to the Full Evaluation's official Workforce Readiness Level, and not a certificate-eligible output

Full MeritLense Evaluation

  • Comprehensive assessment across the five approved Workforce Readiness dimensions: Safety Awareness, Hygiene & Standards, Communication Ability, Practical Task Execution, and Behavioral Indicators
  • Detailed structured report + official Workforce Readiness Level + candidate comparison (per package)
  • AI-Led or Employer-Led assessment path, as applicable to the selected package

Services included across all packages

  • Multilingual dashboards
  • Technical support and account configuration
  • Verifiable digital evaluation certificate, issued only where the linked report satisfies the applicable eligibility requirements (Full Evaluation)

The Platform does not participate in recruitment, candidate selection, or legal representation. The final hiring decision remains the sole responsibility of the Second Party. The Platform may not be used to evaluate academic or specialized professional roles. Use is restricted to the operational, domestic, and non-academic workforce.

3. Responsibilities of the Parties

First Party — MeritLense

  • Ensure secure and reliable platform operation
  • Provide technical support and onboarding assistance
  • Protect Second Party and candidate data in accordance with the GDPR and Estonian data protection laws
  • Maintain platform neutrality and full legal compliance
  • Provide objective evaluation reports based on neutral data and algorithms
  • Notify the Second Party of any material changes to services or pricing at least 14 days in advance

Second Party — Individual / Household Employer

  • Provide accurate information about the candidate to be evaluated and the nature of the work required
  • Obtain explicit consent from the candidate to participate in the evaluation process before entering their data
  • Comply with applicable domestic labor, privacy, and data protection laws in their country
  • Not use evaluation results for discriminatory purposes, unlawful exclusion, or any purpose contrary to the candidate's rights
  • Use the Platform for personal household purposes only, and not for resale or commercial recruitment
  • Maintain the confidentiality of login credentials and not share the account with third parties

4. Consumer Protection and Individual Rights

As an individual (consumer), the Second Party is entitled to the following non-waivable rights:

  • The right to full information about the service and pricing before completing any purchase
  • The right to receive a copy of this Agreement in digital format at any time
  • The right to deletion of personal data from the Platform upon termination of the Agreement, pursuant to the GDPR
  • The right to file a complaint or request a review of any decision related to service quality
  • The right to know how candidate data is processed and used within the Platform

5. Candidate Data and Informed Consent

The Second Party acknowledges that it is legally responsible for obtaining explicit and informed consent from the candidate before conducting any evaluation.

  • Candidate data is processed in accordance with MeritLense's Privacy Policy, aligned with the GDPR
  • The Platform does not share candidate data with third parties without explicit consent
  • The candidate has the right at any time to access, correct, or request deletion of their data
  • Upon expiry of the associated evaluation, personally identifying candidate data (name, contact details, photo / ID image) is deleted or anonymized. The underlying assessment record (readiness level, dates, certificate status) is retained in de-identified form for audit, verification, and legal record-keeping purposes, consistent with the Data Retention Period in MeritLense's Privacy Policy
  • Evaluation results are shared with the Second Party (individual) and the candidate only — transfer to other parties is not permitted

7. Refund Policy (B2C)

As the Second Party is an individual (consumer), the following refund policy applies, based on actual usage of the purchased package:

Accepted refund cases

  • Zero consumption on the purchase — no Assessment Slot has been started and no Points have been consumed. A full refund applies regardless of how much time has passed since purchase, since Assessment Slots and Points do not expire
  • A confirmed billing, payment, or platform error by MeritLense
  • A material breach by the First Party of its contractual obligations

Non-accepted refund cases

  • Any consumption has occurred — at least one Assessment Slot has been started or any Points have been consumed on the purchase, regardless of how much of the package remains unused
  • Second Party dissatisfaction with candidate results (results reflect actual performance)

Refund requests must be submitted in writing to info@meritlense.com with the order number and reason. If approved, refunds will be processed within 14 business days.
