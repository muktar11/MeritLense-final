1. Purpose and Platform Role

MeritLense provides structured workforce-readiness assessment and decision-support services. AI may assist with evidence extraction and processing; assessment outputs are determined through the Platform’s approved assessment logic, rule configuration, and applicable role configuration.

MeritLense is not a recruitment agency, manpower supplier, employment broker, employer, immigration adviser, licensing authority, or legal representative. MeritLense does not select or hire candidates and does not guarantee employment, future performance, visa or immigration approval, professional licensing, or any hiring outcome. The final hiring and employment decision remains solely with the Second Party and, where applicable, the legally responsible or licensed parties.

2. Scope of Services

The services available depend on the package selected and the role configuration supported by the Platform.

Screening Evaluation

  • A preliminary assessment intended to support an initial proceed / no-proceed review.
  • Identity and foundational assessment controls may be included where applicable to the selected assessment.
  • Any preliminary readiness signal is decision-support information only and is not equivalent to an official Full Evaluation Workforce Readiness Level and is not certificate-eligible unless the Platform expressly states otherwise.

Full MeritLense Evaluation

  • A structured assessment against the competencies applicable to the selected role configuration. MeritLense maintains a canonical competency framework, while individual competencies may be Required, Optional, or Not Applicable depending on the approved role profile.
  • A structured assessment report and an official Workforce Readiness Level generated under the applicable assessment logic.
  • A verifiable digital assessment certificate may be issued only where the linked assessment satisfies the Platform’s applicable certificate-eligibility rules.
  • Candidate comparison or other features are provided only where included in the selected package.

A competency marked Not Applicable for a role is not treated as failed merely because it is not assessed. Assessment coverage and readiness are determined under the applicable role configuration.
