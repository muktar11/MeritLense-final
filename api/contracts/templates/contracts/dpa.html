Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Version {{ version }} Effective Date: 2026-09-12 Generated {{ generated_at }} Jurisdiction: Estonia (EU)

Annex to the B2B Services Agreement. Governs MeritLense's processing of personal data on behalf of the Customer, {{ company_name }}.

1. Purpose

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of and supplements the B2B Services Agreement entered into between MeritLense and the Customer regarding the use of MeritLense's pre-employment workforce readiness assessment services. This DPA, together with its Annexes, defines the conditions under which MeritLense processes Personal Data on behalf of the Customer in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), applicable Estonian data protection laws, and any other applicable data protection regulations in the Customer's jurisdiction.

Order of documents: in the event of any conflict regarding data protection matters not otherwise resolved by Section 21 (Liability), this DPA and its Annexes shall take precedence over the B2B Services Agreement to the extent necessary to satisfy applicable data protection law.

MeritLense's Privacy Policy is referenced in this DPA for general informational and descriptive purposes only. It is not a binding contractual document, does not form part of this order of precedence, and does not modify, supplement, or override the obligations set out in this DPA or the B2B Services Agreement.

2. Definitions

  • "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (GDPR Article 4(1)).
  • "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data (GDPR Article 4(2)).
  • "Controller" means the party that determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data.
  • "Processor" means the party that processes Personal Data on behalf of, and under the documented instructions of, a Controller.
  • "Data Subject" means the individual to whom Personal Data relates, including candidates evaluated through the Platform.
  • "Subprocessor" means any third party engaged by MeritLense to assist in providing the Services, as listed in Annex B.
  • "Personal Data Breach" has the meaning given under GDPR Article 4(12).
  • "Special Category Data" means Personal Data described in GDPR Article 9.
  • "Anonymized" means Personal Data that has been processed such that the Data Subject is no longer identifiable, and which is no longer considered Personal Data. "Pseudonymized" means Personal Data processed such that it can no longer be attributed to a Data Subject without additional information kept separately; pseudonymized data remains Personal Data under GDPR.

3. Roles of the Parties

For the processing of candidate Personal Data in connection with the assessment services provided under the B2B Services Agreement, the Customer acts as Controller and MeritLense acts as Processor.

For processing activities where MeritLense determines the purposes and means independently — including public certificate verification (Section 19), platform security logs, aggregated analytics, and legal recordkeeping — MeritLense may act as an independent Controller, and such processing is governed by MeritLense's Privacy Policy and applicable law, rather than by this DPA.

4. Scope and Instructions

MeritLense shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Customer, including those set out in the B2B Services Agreement, this DPA, and Annex A, unless otherwise required by applicable law.

MeritLense shall promptly inform the Customer if, in its reasonable opinion, an instruction from the Customer infringes applicable data protection law.

5. Description of Processing

The subject matter, duration, nature, and purpose of processing, together with the categories of Data Subjects and categories of Personal Data processed under this DPA, are set out in Annex A (Description of Processing).

6. Categories of Personal Data

6.1 Candidate Information

  • Name (used for identity verification and for display on the issued certificate)
  • Nationality, contact information, and other employment-related information, where required for the Services
  • Assessment responses (voice transcripts — deleted or anonymized / pseudonymized per Annex E)
  • Evaluation outcomes (score, readiness indicator — retained per the schedule in Annex E)

6.2 Technical Data

  • IP address, device information, browser information
  • Session logs and audit events

6.3 Verification Data

  • Identity verification status (pass / fail)
  • Underlying identity documents or images: retained for 90 days following completion of the associated identity verification, then automatically deleted

6.4 Special Category Data

MeritLense does not intentionally collect Special Category Data. Where a candidate voluntarily provides Special Category Data within an assessment response, MeritLense shall process such data only to the extent necessary for the provision of the Services and in accordance with the Customer's documented instructions and applicable law.

6.5 Certificate & Verification Data

  • Certificate identifier, certificate status, issue date, and supersession / revocation status
  • Public verification reference

Public disclosure of this data is limited as described in Section 19.

7. Customer Responsibilities

The Customer represents and warrants that:

  • All Personal Data has been collected lawfully with an appropriate legal basis
  • Appropriate candidate consent has been obtained where required by applicable law
  • Data Subjects have been informed of the processing activities and their rights
  • The Customer has a lawful basis for processing Personal Data under GDPR Article 6

The Customer remains solely responsible for determining the legality of data collection and use in its jurisdiction, including any GCC-applicable regulations.

8. MeritLense Responsibilities

MeritLense shall:

  • Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Customer
  • Maintain confidentiality of Personal Data and restrict access to authorized personnel only
  • Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures (Section 13, Annex D)
  • Assist the Customer in responding to lawful Data Subject requests where reasonably required (Section 12)
  • Notify the Customer of any confirmed Personal Data Breach without undue delay (Section 14)

9. AI-Assisted Processing

AI-assisted processing may be used to support processing activities such as transcription, translation, and evidence extraction from candidate responses. AI outputs do not independently constitute an employment decision — MeritLense's evaluation methodology applies defined evaluation rules, and the Customer remains solely responsible for employment decisions. This is consistent with MeritLense's operating principle: AI Assists — Rules Decide — Employers Decide.

  • MeritLense shall not use Customer Personal Data to train, fine-tune, or improve general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine learning models for the benefit of MeritLense or any third party, unless expressly authorized by the Customer in writing and supported by an appropriate legal basis
  • Customer Personal Data shall not be used to train or improve models for the benefit of other customers

Personal Data submitted through the Services is not used by MeritLense's service providers to train or improve their general-purpose models, in accordance with each provider's published data use policies applicable to business / API accounts.

10. Subprocessors

MeritLense may engage Subprocessors to support service delivery. Approved Subprocessors are listed in Annex B.

  • Approved Subprocessors as of this version of the DPA are listed in Annex B
  • The Customer provides general authorization for MeritLense's engagement of the Subprocessors listed in Annex B, as updated from time to time
  • MeritLense shall notify the Customer of any intended addition or replacement of Subprocessors, giving the Customer a reasonable opportunity to object on legitimate data protection grounds
  • Subprocessors are bound by data protection obligations no less protective than those set out in this DPA
  • MeritLense remains liable to the Customer for the acts and omissions of its Subprocessors to the same extent MeritLense would be liable if performing the services directly

11. International Data Transfers

Where Personal Data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA), MeritLense shall implement an appropriate transfer mechanism required under GDPR Article 46 — such as an adequacy decision, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), or another lawful transfer mechanism — as further detailed in Annex C.

Certain Subprocessors listed in Annex B are located outside the EEA, including in the United States. MeritLense shall ensure that any such transfer is subject to an appropriate safeguard in accordance with GDPR Chapter V, including Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable, as set out in Annex C.

12. Data Subject Rights

MeritLense shall assist the Customer, taking into account the nature of processing, in responding to Data Subject requests relating to:

  • Right of Access (GDPR Article 15)
  • Right to Rectification (GDPR Article 16)
  • Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten (GDPR Article 17)
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (GDPR Article 18)
  • Right to Object (GDPR Article 21)
  • Right to Data Portability (GDPR Article 20)

MeritLense shall not respond substantively to Data Subject requests except as instructed by the Customer, unless required by applicable law. The Customer remains responsible for determining and communicating the response to the Data Subject.

13. Security Measures

MeritLense implements security controls including:

  • Encryption of Personal Data in transit using TLS
  • Encryption of Personal Data at rest: Implemented (AES-256)
  • Role-based access control (RBAC)
  • Authentication and authorization controls
  • Comprehensive audit logging
  • Infrastructure monitoring (24/7)
  • Secure hosting environments
  • Periodic security assessments, vulnerability management, and testing of relevant technical and organizational controls
  • Encrypted backups, backup retention, and disaster recovery procedures
  • Confidentiality obligations and data protection / security training for authorized personnel

Full detail is set out in Annex D (Technical & Organizational Security Measures).

14. Personal Data Breach

In the event of a confirmed Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data, MeritLense shall notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of the breach, and shall:

  • Provide the information then available regarding the nature, scope, and likely consequences of the incident, supplementing it as further information becomes available
  • Describe mitigation and remediation measures undertaken or proposed
  • Cooperate to support the Customer's regulatory notification obligations under GDPR Article 33

15. Data Retention / Return / Deletion

MeritLense shall retain Personal Data only for as long as necessary to provide the Services, comply with the Customer's documented instructions, fulfil applicable legal obligations, and satisfy the retention schedule in Annex E. Unless otherwise specified in the B2B Services Agreement or required by law, MeritLense's default retention period is up to 24 months from the date of the last relevant interaction.

  • Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, personally identifying candidate data is deleted or anonymized where technically and legally feasible; otherwise it is retained in pseudonymized form subject to applicable data protection requirements
  • Upon termination of the B2B Services Agreement, the Customer may elect to have Customer Personal Data returned or deleted, except to the extent MeritLense is required by law to retain it

16. DPIA & Regulatory Assistance

MeritLense shall reasonably assist the Customer with information necessary for data protection impact assessments and consultations with supervisory authorities, where required by applicable law.

17. Audit Rights

MeritLense shall make available to the Customer information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, and shall allow and contribute to reasonable audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or an auditor mandated by the Customer, subject to:

  • Reasonable prior written notice
  • Audits conducted during normal business hours
  • Respect for confidentiality obligations owed to other customers, and no access to other customers' data
  • Preference for remote evidence review where practicable
  • The Customer bearing its own costs of conducting the audit

18. Confidentiality

Personnel authorized to access Personal Data are subject to confidentiality obligations and receive appropriate data protection and security training.

19. Public Certificate Verification

Where a certificate issued through the Platform is verified via a public verification reference or QR code, the information disclosed through the verification interface is limited to that necessary to confirm authenticity — such as the certificate identifier, status, issue date, role / profile, and verification confirmation — and does not disclose candidate assessment scores, competency details, or other Personal Data beyond what is necessary for verification.

As set out in Section 3, this processing is carried out by MeritLense as an independent Controller in accordance with MeritLense's Privacy Policy, and is not governed by this DPA.

20. Automated Decision-Making

MeritLense does not make automated decisions on behalf of the Customer that produce legal effects or similarly significant effects concerning candidates. AI-assisted processing may support evidence extraction and evaluation, while final Readiness outcomes are determined according to MeritLense's defined evaluation methodology, and the Customer remains solely responsible for employment decisions.

21. Liability

Each party shall remain responsible for its own compliance with applicable data protection laws.

In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the B2B Services Agreement regarding data protection matters, this DPA shall prevail to the extent necessary to satisfy applicable data protection law.

Nothing in this DPA shall expand either party's liability beyond the limits established in the B2B Services Agreement.

22. Governing Law

This DPA shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of Estonia and applicable European Union data protection regulations, without prejudice to any mandatory data protection requirements applicable to the Customer or Data Subjects in the jurisdiction in which processing occurs. Any disputes arising from this DPA shall be subject to the competent courts of Estonia unless otherwise agreed by the parties in writing.

23. Contact

For all data protection matters relating to this DPA, please contact:

  • MeritLense Platform — Estonia
