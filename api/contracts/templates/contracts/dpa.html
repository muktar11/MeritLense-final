This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the B2B Agreement between MeritLense (the "Processor") and {{ company_name }} (the "Controller"), and governs MeritLense's processing of personal data on the Controller's behalf.

1. Subject Matter and Duration

MeritLense processes candidate and employer personal data solely to provide the evaluation and assessment services described in the B2B Agreement, for the duration of that Agreement.

2. Nature and Purpose of Processing

Processing includes collection, storage, AI-assisted evaluation, and reporting of candidate assessment data submitted by the Controller or its candidates.

3. Data Subject Rights

MeritLense will assist the Controller in responding to data subject requests (access, rectification, erasure, portability) in accordance with applicable data protection law, including GDPR.

4. Security Measures

MeritLense maintains appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

5. Sub-processors

MeritLense may engage sub-processors to support service delivery and will ensure equivalent data protection obligations are imposed on any sub-processor.
