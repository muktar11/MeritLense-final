1. Purpose

This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) forms part of and supplements the B2B Services Agreement between MeritLense OÜ (“MeritLense”) and the customer identified in the applicable order, account, or service agreement (“Customer”). It governs MeritLense’s processing of Personal Data on behalf of the Customer in connection with MeritLense workforce readiness assessment services and is intended to satisfy the applicable requirements of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”), including Article 28.

2. Definitions

Capitalized terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the B2B Services Agreement. “Personal Data”, “Processing”, “Controller”, “Processor”, “Data Subject”, “Personal Data Breach”, and “Special Categories of Personal Data” have the meanings given in the GDPR. “Subprocessor” means a third party engaged by MeritLense to process Customer Personal Data on MeritLense’s behalf in providing the Services.

3. Roles of the Parties

For candidate Personal Data processed to provide the Customer’s assessment services, the Customer acts as Controller and MeritLense acts as Processor. Where MeritLense independently determines the purposes and means of a separate processing activity, including platform security, legal recordkeeping, service administration, and limited public certificate/report verification, MeritLense acts as an independent Controller for that activity under its Privacy Policy and applicable law.

4. Processing Instructions

MeritLense shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Customer, including instructions contained in the B2B Services Agreement, this DPA, the Customer’s use of the Services, and Annex A, unless processing is required by applicable law. MeritLense shall inform the Customer if, in its reasonable opinion, an instruction infringes applicable data protection law, unless prohibited from doing so by law.

5. Customer Responsibilities

The Customer is responsible for establishing and maintaining a lawful basis for the Personal Data it provides or causes to be processed, providing required notices to Data Subjects, obtaining consent where consent is legally required, and ensuring that its instructions and use of the Services comply with applicable law. The Customer remains responsible for employment, recruitment, engagement, licensing, immigration, and other decisions concerning candidates.

6. Confidentiality and Access

MeritLense shall ensure that persons authorized to process Customer Personal Data are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations and that access is limited to persons and systems requiring it for legitimate service, security, support, or legal purposes.

7. Security

Taking into account the state of the art, implementation costs, the nature, scope, context and purposes of Processing, and the risks to Data Subjects, MeritLense shall maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect Customer Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to Personal Data. Measures may include access controls, authentication controls, encryption in transit where appropriate, logging, secure hosting controls, confidentiality controls, and proportionate security review and incident-management practices. MeritLense may update its security measures provided that the overall level of protection is not materially reduced.

8. AI-Assisted Processing and Methodology Confidentiality

The Services may use AI-assisted tools to support functions such as transcription, translation, language processing, and evidence extraction. AI-assisted outputs do not independently make an employment decision. MeritLense applies its assessment methodology and configured evaluation rules to produce assessment outputs, while the Customer remains solely responsible for employment decisions. Nothing in this DPA requires MeritLense to disclose proprietary prompts, algorithms, scoring logic, rule-engine configuration, indicator libraries, source code, model architecture, trade secrets, or other confidential methodology except to the limited extent disclosure is required by applicable law or a competent supervisory authority. Customer Personal Data shall not be used by MeritLense to train a general-purpose model for the benefit of other customers unless the Customer expressly authorizes that use in writing and a lawful basis exists.

9. Subprocessors

The Customer gives MeritLense general written authorization to engage Subprocessors necessary to provide the Services. MeritLense shall impose data protection obligations on Subprocessors appropriate to the processing they perform and remains responsible for their performance to the extent required by Article 28 GDPR. MeritLense shall make its current material Subprocessor information available to the Customer and shall provide reasonable notice of material additions or replacements where required, allowing the Customer to raise reasonable objections based on legitimate data-protection grounds.

10. International Data Transfers

Where Customer Personal Data is transferred from the EEA to a country or recipient not covered by an applicable adequacy decision, MeritLense shall rely on a lawful transfer mechanism available under Chapter V GDPR, as applicable to the relevant transfer. This may include the European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, an applicable adequacy framework or certification mechanism, or another lawful safeguard. Where required, MeritLense shall implement supplementary measures appropriate to the transfer. The applicable mechanism may be provided through the relevant provider’s data processing terms or other binding contractual arrangement. Nothing in this DPA represents that a particular transfer mechanism applies where it is not legally or operationally applicable.

11. Data Subject Requests

Taking into account the nature of the Processing, MeritLense shall provide reasonable assistance to the Customer with requests from Data Subjects relating to Customer Personal Data, including requests concerning access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability where applicable. Unless required by law, MeritLense shall not determine the substantive response on the Customer’s behalf.

12. Personal Data Breach

MeritLense shall notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data and shall provide information reasonably available to MeritLense that the Customer requires to meet applicable notification obligations. MeritLense’s notification of an incident does not constitute an admission of fault or liability.

13. Assistance with Compliance

Taking into account the nature of Processing and the information available to MeritLense, MeritLense shall provide reasonable assistance required under Article 28 GDPR in relation to security, breach obligations, data protection impact assessments, and prior consultations, where applicable. Assistance that requires material work beyond the ordinary Services may be subject to reasonable fees where permitted by the B2B Services Agreement and applicable law.

14. Return, Deletion and Retention

Customer Personal Data shall be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, comply with documented Customer instructions, maintain legitimate security and audit records, satisfy applicable legal obligations, and apply the retention schedule in Annex D. On termination of the Services, MeritLense shall, at the Customer’s choice and subject to applicable law and technical feasibility, return or delete Customer Personal Data processed solely on the Customer’s behalf. Data that MeritLense is independently required or permitted to retain as Controller is outside that return/deletion instruction and remains subject to applicable law and MeritLense’s Privacy Policy.

15. Audit and Compliance Information

MeritLense shall make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 GDPR. Audits must be proportionate, relevant to the Services, subject to reasonable prior written notice, conducted during normal business hours, and designed to protect the security, confidentiality, intellectual property, trade secrets, and data of MeritLense and other customers. Remote documentation review shall be preferred where it reasonably satisfies the audit purpose. No audit right grants access to source code, proprietary assessment methodology, other customers’ data, or information that would materially compromise platform security, except where disclosure is strictly required by applicable law or a competent authority.

16. Public Verification

Where MeritLense provides a public certificate or report verification function, the public interface shall be limited to information reasonably necessary to confirm authenticity and status. Assessment scores, detailed competency evidence, candidate responses, and other non-public assessment information shall not be made public through the verification interface unless a separate lawful basis and appropriate notice apply.

17. Automated Decision-Making

MeritLense does not make the Customer’s final employment decision. Assessment outputs are decision-support information produced under the MeritLense assessment methodology. The Customer remains responsible for determining how assessment outputs are used in its employment or engagement process and for any human review required by applicable law.

18. Liability

Each party remains responsible for its own compliance with applicable data protection law. Nothing in this DPA expands either party’s liability beyond the limitations and allocation of liability in the B2B Services Agreement, except to the extent such limitation is prohibited by applicable law.

19. Order of Precedence

For data protection matters, this DPA prevails over conflicting terms in the B2B Services Agreement only to the extent necessary to comply with applicable data protection law. MeritLense’s Privacy Policy does not modify the contractual allocation of Controller/Processor obligations under this DPA.

20. Governing Law

This DPA is governed by the laws of the Republic of Estonia and applicable European Union data protection law, without prejudice to mandatory data protection requirements that apply independently to a party or processing activity. Disputes relating to this DPA are subject to the competent courts of Estonia, unless mandatory law requires otherwise.

21. Electronic Acceptance and Language

This DPA may be accepted electronically as part of the Customer’s onboarding, order, or use of the Services and has the same effect as written acceptance to the extent permitted by law. If MeritLense provides a translation of this DPA, the English version is the controlling version in the event of inconsistency or conflict in interpretation, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
